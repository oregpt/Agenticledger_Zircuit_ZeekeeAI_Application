# 03. Economics and Token

## TLDR Quick Snapshot

Focus on immediate utility, not token mechanics—with future vision for AI agent tokenization on Zircuit.
* **Current Model**: Value through reduced support costs, faster onboarding, tip system for guide creators
* **No Token Required**: Grant period focused on infrastructure and adoption
* **Future Vision**: AI agent ownership tokens + licensing tokens for specialized agents
* **Alignment**: Projects save costs, users get better support, ecosystem gains knowledge layer

---

## Protocol Economics

We're focused on building useful infrastructure first, not launching a token. The value comes from helping projects save money on support, getting users onboarded faster, and preserving knowledge that usually gets lost in Discord. Builders benefit from reduced operational overhead as AI agents handle repetitive support inquiries. Users get instant, accurate guidance available 24/7. The guides platform includes a tip system where community members can reward helpful guide creators in ZRC or stablecoins, creating direct value flow to contributors.

## Future Tokenization Vision

While not part of the initial grant scope, we're exploring long-term concepts for AI agent tokenization on Zircuit. The vision involves two potential token types:

* **Ownership Tokens:** Representing immutable intellectual property rights for custom AI agents, establishing verifiable creation and ownership records on-chain.

* **Licensing Tokens:** Enabling usage rights for specialized agents, allowing creators to monetize their agents while maintaining control and providing auditable access.

This represents a future exploration of how AI agents can be treated as blockchain-native assets with clear ownership and licensing mechanisms. The immediate focus is on building valuable AI-powered infrastructure and demonstrating adoption. Tokenization would be a natural evolution once the platform proves its worth and gains ecosystem traction.

## Economic Incentive Alignment

The current model aligns incentives through direct utility: projects reduce costs, users receive better support, and the ecosystem benefits from compounding knowledge. The tip system for guide creators provides immediate economic rewards for valuable contributions. As the platform matures, tokenization concepts may introduce additional incentive mechanisms for agent creators, knowledge contributors, and ecosystem participants.
